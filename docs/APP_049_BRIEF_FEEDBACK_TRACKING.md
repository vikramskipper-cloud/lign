# APP 049 — Feedback response and independent verification

Each changes-requested brief decision creates an overall tracked feedback item. Existing decisions are backfilled as open feedback, using their original author/date, without inventing a team response or client verification. Leadership or the original client decision-maker can log specific items against a field, requirement/question, brief link or overall brief in that source revision. The original wording and source identity are immutable.

The internal team can mark open feedback addressed with a recorded response against the current saved draft/version or a current requested/agreed revision. Local edits must be saved first. If the team responds against a draft, creation of the next agreement revision binds the response only when its saved content, shared selected references and attached files still match. Status-only team-review transitions preserve that evidence. Later content edits make it stale and require a refreshed team response. An addressed response alone never means the client verified it.

Only the client who made the original changes-requested decision can independently verify a response, on the exact current requested or agreed revision. A responder cannot verify their own response. Old revision links and stale item versions fail closed. The client can reopen a response with a reason; earlier verification and response events remain in the append-only audit trail. Reopening feedback does not rewrite an agreed brief or automatically start a new revision. Leadership uses the established revision flow for actual brief changes. Agreement does not implicitly verify outstanding feedback items, and individual verification does not substitute for whole-brief agreement.

The tracker names the source revision, target, response revision, actors and dates, with source/response navigation and direct editing links when the current item still exists. Verified items can be hidden/shown. It appears only on the current Review & agree page; saved package content is unaffected. Failed reads expose Retry; stale mutations reload current state. Archived projects reject feedback writes. Raw authenticated writes are revoked, original feedback identity/text is immutable, and audit events cannot be edited or deleted even through service-role table writes.

No email delivery changes are included. Feedback is currently logged from the recorded overall change request or explicitly added specific items; text is not automatically split or interpreted as resolved.

Validation: `tests/access/brief-feedback.mjs` covers automatic capture, source-scoped targets, team/client separation, saved-draft binding, changed-version rejection, exact revision and item concurrency, reopen/audit retention, no implicit verification and raw/cross-project access boundaries. The combined handoff browser journey covers targeted navigation, two independent verifications, agreement, operational reconciliation and historical isolation. All existing unit tests and brief regression journeys remain required.
